02 CORPORATE HISTORY

Corporate History

Encompass Health is the successor to HealthSouth Corporation, which was the subject of a $2.7 billion accounting fraud scandal in 2003 under founder Richard Scrushy.

Year Event

03 DOJ SETTLEMENT

DOJ Settlement

In June 2019, Encompass Health paid $48 million to resolve False Claims Act allegations that its facilities admitted patients who did not meet IRF criteria.

04 REGULATORY RISK

Medicare Dependency and Regulatory Risk

Encompass Health derives 82% of its revenue from Medicare. A 7% site-neutral payment reduction would cost the company approximately $280 million annually.

Structural Conflict

Tarr serves as FAH Treasurer while his company derives 82% of revenue from the Medicare reimbursement rates FAH lobbies to protect. A 7% site-neutral reduction on $4B in Medicare revenue represents approximately $280M in annual exposure.
